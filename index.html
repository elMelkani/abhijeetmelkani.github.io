Abhijeet Melkani

(he/him/his) Postdoctoral researcher abhijeet.melkani [AT] ens-lyon.fr

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Denis Bartolo’s group at ENS Lyon. My research lies at the interface of active matter and biophysics with a focus on collective dynamics. My work combines analytical approaches---including hydrodynamics, kinetic theory, and tools from non-Hermitian and topological dynamics---with data-driven techniques such as machine learning and agent-based simulations. I am currently working on understanding the shape dynamics of wild fish schools.

I received my PhD in Physics from the University of Oregon in 2024, where my dissertation studied activity and non-reciprocity in soft matter using ideas from non-Hermitian physics. Previously, I completed an undergraduate degree in Engineering Physics at IIT Bombay in 2019, working on quantum information and quantum optics.
